    17 source-checked novels added after reviewing the 21 empty destinations. Edition dates, original languages and diaspora or residence connections are documented. Six destinations remain under research. Read the research notes ↗

  4. 18 November 2026

    Book links, covers and short reading notes

    421 direct Goodreads book links added, 55 missing covers supplied, and 647 source-based short reading notes. The atlas now has 690 direct Goodreads links. Unresolved notes and missing cover matches remain explicitly marked. Google Books was tried, but its public API quota was unavailable.

  5. 18 November 2026

    A calmer atlas

    Consistent typography and icons, a periwinkle accent, continent-grouped country navigation and a softer current-country marker. Decorative theme illustrations have been removed.
