Updates

A dated record of changes to the selection, book metadata and browsing experience.

  1. 19 November 2026

    Small improvements for curious readers

    Redundant Goodreads searches are removed wherever a direct book link exists, with edition links taking a quieter role. Search keeps accent-insensitive matching and live counts, adds an always-available clear control while typing, and explains empty results. “Surprise me” offers a random book with its summary and a direct jump to its place in the atlas.

  2. 18 November 2026

    Short, original summaries for every book

    All 775 selections now have individually written, 10–15-word summaries. Concrete premises and subjects replace clipped opening lines; per-book references are collected under Sources. Herman Bang’s unverified English title has also been replaced with Katinka (Ved Vejen).

  3. 18 November 2026

    A softer cover-loading state

    Book-specific gradients hold each cover’s place while lazy-loaded images arrive, then fade into the cover. Failed images retain the gradient, keeping the reading layout stable.

  4. 18 November 2026

    188 more book covers

    The atlas now displays 718 covers, including all five books in Chile. Public Goodreads book metadata and a publisher page supplied identity-checked images, stored locally with lazy loading. The remaining 57 selections retain gradient placeholders until a reliable cover is found.

  5. 18 November 2026

    A clearer mobile atlas

    A single compact toolbar keeps the current country visible, with separate search and navigation controls. About now brings together Overview, Reading routes, Sources and Updates as linked sections. The footer is simpler and the reading layout has more consistent spacing.

  6. 18 November 2026

    60 more original publication dates

    Publisher, library, author and research sources resolved 60 of the 63 remaining date records. There are now 772 first-publication labels, two edition fallbacks and one unverified date. Conflicting records remain open. Garth St Omer’s title has also been corrected from Prisms to Prisnms.

    Follow-up evidence and open questions
  7. 18 November 2026

    Original publication years first

    A review of all 775 entries reduced edition-year fallbacks from 554 to 23. The atlas now shows 712 first-publication labels; 40 dates remain unverified. Goodreads work dates were checked separately from edition dates, with publisher, library and research sources resolving conflicting translations, reprints and composition dates. Eight existing original dates or serial ranges still await fresh confirmation.

  8. 18 November 2026

    More nonfiction discoveries

    New routes through nonfiction

    Seven clearly labelled nonfiction books fill the six remaining empty destinations: memoirs, travel writing, history and investigative reporting.   10. 18 November 2026

    First selections for 15 destinations

    17 source-checked novels added after reviewing the 21 empty destinations. Edition dates, original languages and diaspora or residence connections are documented. Six destinations remain under research. Read the research notes

  11. 18 November 2026

    Book links, covers and short reading notes

    421 direct Goodreads book links added, 55 missing covers supplied, and 647 source-based short reading notes. The atlas now has 690 direct Goodreads links. Unresolved notes and missing cover matches remain explicitly marked. Google Books was tried, but its public API quota was unavailable.

  12. 18 November 2026

    A calmer atlas

    Consistent typography and icons, a periwinkle accent, continent-grouped country navigation and a softer current-country marker. Decorative theme illustrations have been removed.
