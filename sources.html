Where the records come from

Open Library catalogue records support title, author and edition matching. Its Covers API supplies most cover images. A catalogue match is evidence for a record association, not proof of national affiliation, literary quality or translation availability.

Goodreads book pages provide direct discovery links and short, attributed synopsis extracts. Some thematic notes use subjects from matched catalogue editions. These are source-based orientation notes, not reviews written after reading every book.

What has been checked

  • 717 direct Goodreads links: search links remain for unresolved matches. The earlier metadata refresh added 421 title-and-author-matched links; earlier catalogue-supplied links were retained.
  • 530 covers: the earlier metadata refresh added 55 images from Open Library or a verified Goodreads page. Those new image files were checked and stored locally.
  • 678 source-based notes: short extracts or catalogue themes; 97 notes remain unverified. Tags are conservative keyword-based suggestions.
  • 587 edition-record matches in the earlier catalogue audit: an edition date may describe a translation or later printing. 141 dates remain unverified; this is separate from whether an edition record was matched.

Publication dates

Original-language first publication takes priority. An explicitly labelled edition year remains the fallback when the original date is unresolved. Earliest catalogue editions and translation dates are not automatically treated as first publication. Verified original-year sources and the complete date audit distinguish source-checked dates, retained original dates and edition fallbacks.

Limits and next steps

Publisher, genre, language and current availability have not been separately audited for every book. Conflicting or uncertain matches remain open rather than being silently treated as verified.

Google Books was tested on 18 November 2026, but the public API returned a quota error; no Google Books covers were added in that pass. Further data providers are being considered and are not yet part of the collection’s evidence.
