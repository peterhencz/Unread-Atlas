Where the records come from

Open Library catalogue records support title, author and edition matching. Its Covers API supplies most cover images. A catalogue match is evidence for a record association, not proof of national affiliation, literary quality or translation availability.

Goodreads book pages provide direct discovery links and descriptions used alongside publisher, author, library and literary references. The summaries are original reading pointers, not reviews written after reading every book.

Short editorial summaries

All 775 selections (773 distinct books) have individually written, 10–15-word English summaries. Each offers a concrete premise or subject without promotional judgments. They paraphrase checked descriptions and references rather than reproducing opening lines. Sources include publishers, authors, libraries, Goodreads and literary research; these are not claims of having read every book. Country-context notes and theme tags remain separate.

Reading themes

All 775 selections (773 distinct books) have been individually reviewed against book-specific descriptions. Publisher descriptions are preferred; where unavailable, the record identifies author, library, literary, institutional or academic sources. Some sources are brief, so their tags make narrower claims. The atlas summaries alone are not the basis of this review.

A shared vocabulary of 24 themes keeps the labels consistent, with at most two per book. These are editorial orientation cues, not publisher-assigned genres, exhaustive classifications or a claim to have read every book. A passing mention of a war, relative or memory does not automatically justify a tag.

Publication dates

The follow-up review checked all 63 remaining records against other publisher, author, library and research sources, confirming 60 additional original-publication dates. Read the follow-up evidence and decisions. Three records remain open: The End of the Legend (1969 / 1974 conflict), 15 Dias de Regresso (1993 / 1994 conflict), and Lifestyle in the Countryside (only the 1994 edition is established).

All 775 book entries were reviewed on 18 November 2026 (773 distinct title-and-author pairs). 772 show “First published”, two retain an explicitly labelled edition year, and one remains unverified. Of the 772 original dates, 764 have a checked publication source; eight pre-existing original dates or serial ranges are retained without claiming fresh verification.

Original-language first publication takes priority over translations and reprints. Goodreads’s work-level date is checked against the displayed “First published” field and the book’s title and author; its edition-level timestamp is never used for this. Translated titles and author variants are reviewed separately. Earlier catalogue dates and conflicting sources trigger a further check. Publisher bibliographies, original-publication catalogue notes and research sources take precedence where they resolve a discrepancy.

Goodreads is community-maintained and can contain errors. This review found dates for later translations, reprints and even composition recorded as first publication. Unresolved records retain “Edition” or “Date not verified”; an earliest catalogue year is not automatically promoted.

Limits and next steps

Publisher, genre, language and current availability have not been separately audited for every book. Conflicting or uncertain matches remain open rather than being silently treated as verified.

Google Books was tested on 18 November 2026, but the public API returned a quota error; no Google Books covers were added in that pass. Further data providers are being considered and are not yet part of the collection’s evidence.
