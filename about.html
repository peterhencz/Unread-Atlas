A world of stories

Unread Atlas is an independent guide to reading beyond familiar borders. It brings classics, contemporary fiction, memoirs and nonfiction into one growing collection — a place to discover a voice, rather than a definitive book for every country.

Find your way in

Browse a country, search for an author or title, or take a detour through the reading routes. A country’s “Starting point” is an invitation, not a ranking. There is no prescribed order.

A selection with context

The atlas covers 195 countries, with Taiwan and Kosovo as additional literary destinations. Fiction connections follow an author’s origins, cultural affiliations or documented residence, rather than simply where a story is set. A writer may belong to more than one tradition.

Clearly labelled nonfiction offers another way into places with fewer fiction selections. The notes distinguish local voices from outside perspectives; no single book can represent a whole country.

Behind each record

Dates prioritise original publication over later editions. Each book has a short, original summary based on checked descriptions and references, documented under Sources. These are reading pointers, not reviews written after reading every book. Covers can belong to a different translation or edition, and an English title does not guarantee an available English translation.

The Sources tab documents the evidence and remaining uncertainties. Updates records how the selection and the site evolve.
