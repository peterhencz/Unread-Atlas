A place to begin

Unread Atlas is an independent editorial guide to books from around the world, with fiction at its heart. Explore a country, follow an author or take a thematic detour. The selection brings classics, modern voices and contemporary fiction into the same conversation.

Choose your own route

Search the atlas by country, author or book title. Browse the continent navigation or country index to move through the collection. Prefer to start with a feeling or an idea? Reading routes connects books through themes such as memory, migration and family.

A country’s “Starting point” is an editorial invitation, not a ranking. Routes can be read in any order; they are suggestions rather than a syllabus.

A selection, not a complete canon

The atlas includes 195 countries, with Taiwan and Kosovo as additional literary destinations. The selection continues to grow as further suitable books are researched.

Country connections reflect authors’ origins or cultural affiliations, including diaspora writing and, where explicitly stated, sustained residence and cultural affiliation. They do not necessarily describe a book’s setting. Authors can belong to several traditions, and a repeated title still represents one book.

Beyond fiction

Where fiction selections are sparse, clearly labelled Nonfiction books offer another route into a country: memoirs, travel writing, history and investigations. These books may connect through the author’s life or through a documented focus on the country. Local voices and outside perspectives are distinguished explicitly.

The atlas currently contains 761 fiction selections and 14 nonfiction selections. Every destination now has at least one book. An entry does not claim to represent a country’s entire literature or culture.

What a book record tells you

Dates: “First published” is preferred and refers to the original-language publication or serialisation, rather than the translation or cover edition. “Edition” refers to the particular catalogue edition, which may be a later printing or translation. Approximate and unresolved dates are labelled. The latest full-list review leaves 712 first-publication labels, 23 edition fallbacks and 40 unverified dates; eight original dates or serial ranges still await fresh confirmation.

Notes and tags: brief quoted synopsis extracts, source-based paraphrases or catalogue themes help orient you. Tags are brief thematic pointers, not individual critical reviews. 678 books have source-based notes; 97 are explicitly awaiting verification.

Covers and links: 530 books have covers and 717 have direct Goodreads pages. A cover may belong to a different translation or edition. Search links remain where a direct match has not been verified.

English titles are used where identified, but an English title does not guarantee a currently available translation or edition. For the evidence behind individual records, visit Sources; for recent changes, see Updates.
